ROMS vs Moorings

Fixed mooring records around Iceland — ADCP currents and MicroCat temperature/salinity — colocated with the nearest ROMS grid cell at each instrument's actual mean depth. Click a mooring on the map, then pick a deployment, variable and depth.

Select a mooring on the map to see its comparison.

    DS1 (Denmark Strait), HB2–HB4 (Húnaflói) and SL2-2 are moored ADCP (currents) and MicroCat (temperature/salinity) records spanning 2017–2019, maintained by HAFRO. The ROMS daily-average archive used here covers Dec 2017–Dec 2018, so the 2018–2019 deployments only overlap for their first few months.
