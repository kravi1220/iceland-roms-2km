ROMS vs Moorings

In progress

Comparison of the model against fixed mooring records around Iceland is under development. This page will show colocated ROMS time series against the ADCP current and MicroCat temperature/salinity records below, following the same colocation approach used for the Argo float comparison.

DS1 (Denmark Strait), HB2–HB4 (Húnaflói) and SL2-2 are moored ADCP (currents) and MicroCat (temperature/salinity) records spanning 2017–2019, maintained by HAFRO.

Next steps

Colocating ROMS output with each mooring's position and instrument depths, then comparing time series and computing skill metrics analogous to the Argo Taylor diagrams, is planned as a follow-up to this site. Check the GitHub repository for progress.
