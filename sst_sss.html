ROMS vs Satellite SST & SSS

Daily sea surface temperature and salinity from the model, compared side by side with satellite observations. Pick a date to see that day's field.

Sources & methodology

ROMS surface temperature/salinity are daily snapshots from the model's history archive (Dec 2017–Dec 2018). Satellite SST is the NOAA daily optimum-interpolation AVHRR product; satellite SSS is the CMEMS multi-sensor (SMOS/SMAP) sea surface salinity product. Each satellite field is matched to the nearest available day to the selected ROMS date (usually the same day). Comparisons are shown every 3 days across the model record.

Data sources

  • Satellite SST: NOAA daily Optimum Interpolation SST from AVHRR (OISST) — NOAA NCEI.
  • Satellite SSS: Copernicus Marine Service (CMEMS) multi-observations global ocean sea surface salinity (SMOS/SMAP based) — MULTIOBS_GLO_PHY_SSS_L4_MY_015_015.
  • Coastlines: Natural Earth 10 m.
