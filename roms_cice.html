ROMS-CICE Coupled Sea-Ice Model

Under development · coming soon

A coupled ocean–sea-ice configuration of the Iceland 2 km model, adding a dynamic–thermodynamic sea-ice component to ROMS for the northern part of the domain.

Cartoon illustration of sea-ice floes around Iceland; not model output.

What is being developed

The current Iceland ROMS configuration is ocean-only. Sea ice matters for the region — polar water and drift ice from the East Greenland Current reach the Denmark Strait and the north Icelandic shelf, and ice cover controls air–sea exchange of heat and momentum and freshwater input. We are coupling ROMS to the CICE sea-ice model, which represents ice thickness distribution, drift, growth and melt, so that ocean and ice evolve together.

Planned

  • Coupled ROMS–CICE simulation on the existing 2 km Iceland grid.
  • Validation of ice extent and concentration against satellite products.
  • Effects of sea ice on Denmark Strait and north Icelandic shelf hydrography.
  • Results published here alongside the existing Argo, mooring and satellite comparisons.
This work is in progress; no results are available yet. Check back later.
